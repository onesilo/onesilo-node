# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want silo-node to be a project where people are judged on their work and
treated decently while they do it. Everyone participating — issues, pull
requests, discussions, reviews — is expected to help keep it that way,
regardless of their experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are technical, and most
  technical disagreements are resolved by evidence.
- Critique code and arguments, not people.
- Accept that maintainers will decline things. A rejected PR is not a
  judgment of the contributor, and "no" is often about scope or the
  dependency budget rather than quality.
- Be direct. Blunt technical feedback is welcome; contempt is not.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or demeaning comments about protected characteristics.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately filing issues in bad
  faith.
- Posting an unfixed vulnerability publicly instead of following
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This applies in every project space — the repository, issues, pull
requests, discussions — and when someone is representing the project
elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues to **conduct@onesilo.com**. Reports stay confidential.
Maintainers will look into it and respond as they judge appropriate, which
may mean editing or removing content, or blocking an account. Maintainers
who do not follow this document are subject to it as much as anyone else.
